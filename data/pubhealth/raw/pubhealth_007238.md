Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
This is a story about a new device coming to market for menstrual pain. The story is a great example of how the media should deal with claims made about new devices. With a dose of healthy skepticism, the story provides the reader with a complete discourse on the merits of the device and of TENS (transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation) in general. We welcomed that skepticism across the board and appreciated the grounding comments of an independent source. Bravo. Perimenstrual pain is a common problem affecting most premenopausal women. So, the availability of a device that is safe and effective in relieving symptoms would be welcomed.
