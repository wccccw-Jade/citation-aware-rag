# PubHealth Item 6344

Dataset source id: 6366
Document id: 25975

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
A California state lawmaker claimed there is “no science or data to suggest that kids are at risk from COVID.” Medical experts say that’s just plain wrong. There are studies that have examined children and found they are at risk of COVID-19, albeit less risk than adults. There’s also a lack of data and uncertainty about how much children could spread the coronavirus among teachers, family members and the wider community once schools reopen.
