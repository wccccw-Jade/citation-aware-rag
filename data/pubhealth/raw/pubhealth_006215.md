Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
"""The CDC has not “confirmed” any mortality rate for COVID-19. The Facebook posts draw on the low-end of a range of estimates the CDC published in May saying the fatality rate for those showing symptoms was between 0.2% and 1%, with a """"best estimate"""" of 0.4%. The estimated overall mortality rate of those with and without symptoms would be 0.26%. Several health experts said they project the mortality rate is on the higher-end of the CDC’s estimated range, closer to 1%, which is 10 times more lethal than the seasonal flu."""
