# PubHealth Item 5675

Dataset source id: 5691
Document id: 10294

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
This was a trial of the drugs tenofovir and emtricitabine (in the combination sold as Truvada) in 12 monkeys, lasting 4 months. However, data on the effectiveness of treatment in humans is lacking. Even replicate trials in non-human primates is lacking. While it is true that this treatment shows promise, data supporting the use of this treatment in humans remains to be established. Further – the basis of the main portion of this article derives from an oral presentation at a meeting, the results of which have not undergone peer review. Woloshin and Schwartz published an article in JAMA a few years ago entitled, “Media Coverage of Scientific Meetings: Too Much, Too Soon?” That theme might appropriately be applied to this story. The enthusiasm engendered in this story may be premature and – worse – misleading.
