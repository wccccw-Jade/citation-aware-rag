# PubHealth Item 3534

Dataset source id: 3544
Document id: 37926

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
Ben Shapiro’s August 2020 critique of the song “WAP” led to widespread mocking on Twitter, and on August 21 2020, a tweet was attributed to Shapiro. In it, he purportedly said he and his wife “both know there are much more important things in a marriage than sexual satisfaction.” However, the first sharer of the screenshot circulating later commented that the tweet was satire — a claim supported by the lack of any indication Shapiro had tweeted and then deleted that particular comment.
