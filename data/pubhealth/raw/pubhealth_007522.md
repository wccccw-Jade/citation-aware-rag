# PubHealth Item 7522

Dataset source id: 7546
Document id: 26453

Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
Some countries in Asia that at one time appeared to have had the spread of Covid-19 under control have seen an uptick in cases after a relaxing of social distancing measures or an influx of travelers from elsewhere. In some places, authorities have had to reimpose social distancing measures to contain a second wave of cases.
