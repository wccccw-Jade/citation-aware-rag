# PubHealth Item 6048

Dataset source id: 6067

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
This story performed cheerleading for local research in a number of ways. It said the procedure: May revolutionize cardiac care; Could save and improve thousands of lives (no source cited for this projection); Has vast potential. But – for the new use described in the story – it’s only been tested in animals. No discussion of evidence, of costs, of harms. Only anecdotes. Incomplete discussion of alternative options for the problem in question. A reality check on a story like this: If the research came out of Ann Arbor or Columbus instead of Indianapolis, do you really think it would get this kind of play? Would it have been covered at all? If not, why not? And then why is it worthy of such hyperlocal hyperbole just because it did involve an Indianapolis physician?
