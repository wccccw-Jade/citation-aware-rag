Evidence and explanation:
Statistics from the Federal Bureau of Investigation show that 89 U.S. law enforcement officials were killed on the job in 2019, 48 of them stemming from criminal acts. Also in 2019, police killed 999 Americans, according to a database from the Washington Post. Black Americans were more than twice as likely to be shot and killed by police than their white counterparts If 48 police officers were killed, and 999 deaths occured at the hands of police, that puts Larson’s claim on track.
