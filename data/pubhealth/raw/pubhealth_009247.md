# PubHealth Item 9247

Dataset source id: 9274
Document id: 11600

Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
Maybe somebody is paying attention to our criteria checklist. As you read this story, one by one, they are addressed (except for one). It is important to remind consumers that there is good evidence for what stool tests – especially the newer versions – can accomplish. From a public health perspective – looking at population-wide benefits – they make a great deal of sense because you can reach large numbers of people at low cost. Oftentimes, colonoscopy dominates the discussion about colon cancer screening, when, in fact, there are many reasons to shine more of the attention on the stool test.
