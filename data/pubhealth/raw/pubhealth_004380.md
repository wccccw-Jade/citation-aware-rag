# PubHealth Item 4380

Dataset source id: 4390
Document id: 26541

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
The federal stimulus bill in response to the coronavirus outbreak does not specify unemployment payments be made at a rate equivalent to $24 per hour. A $600-per-week federal boost in unemployment payments means some unemployed workers will receive the equivalent of $24 an hour, but that depends on how much their state pays for basic unemployment benefits. The stimulus contains no provision to raise pay for people still on the job.
