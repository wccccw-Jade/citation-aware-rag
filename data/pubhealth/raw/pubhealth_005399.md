# PubHealth Item 5399

Dataset source id: 5412
Document id: 29344

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: A preliminary and limited study demonstrated a correlation between the early stages of Alzheimer’s and the reduced ability to detect odor in a person’s left nostril compared to their right, utilizing peanut butter as the source of odor. What's false: This study, which was published in 2013 and had a sample size of only 94 patients (of which only 18 had been diagnosed with Alzheimer's), was not large enough to be conclusive; later attempts to replicate its results have failed or were based on similarly limited sample sizes.
