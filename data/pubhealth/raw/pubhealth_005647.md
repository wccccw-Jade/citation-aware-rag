# PubHealth Item 5647

Dataset source id: 5663
Document id: 26735

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
A popular image claims to show a news reporter staging a shot of empty grocery store shelves. But the photo is a hoax from Romania. The trend of stockpiling groceries started in China after the coronavirus outbreak began. Stores in Europe and the United States have been similarly affected. There is no evidence here that the media has staged its coverage of the coronavirus outbreak to cause undue panic.
