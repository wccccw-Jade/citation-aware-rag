# PubHealth Item 2588

Dataset source id: 2595
Document id: 18163

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
"""Campos-Duffy said """"upwards of 90 percent"""" of women seeking an abortion decide not to have an abortion after seeing an ultrasound. Research indicates that some women seeking an abortion change their mind after having an ultrasound. But, while one anti-abortion organization claims a figure of nearly 90 percent for its ultrasound program among a certain group of women, there is no solid evidence to back Campos-Duffy’s sweeping claim."""
