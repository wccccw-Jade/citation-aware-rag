# PubHealth Item 5678

Dataset source id: 5694

Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
It’s not often you see a story that so simply explains possible confounders in research. This story hammered the point: the findings don’t prove that fiber itself lowers cancer risk the results can identify associations but can’t tell what will happen if people change behavior it’s impossible to rule out that big fiber eaters had healthier habits overall that would cut their risk.
