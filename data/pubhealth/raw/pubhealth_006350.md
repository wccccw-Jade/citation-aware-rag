# PubHealth Item 6350

Dataset source id: 6372
Document id: 29295

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: Authorities in Harris County, Texas did spray for mosquitoes using the insecticide naled, which can be considered a neurotoxin, though these effects are dose dependent. What's false: Authorities did not warn residents to don gas masks; the risk of neurotoxic damage in humans is limited to exposure to naled at a far higher concentration than would be experienced with ultra-low volume aerial spraying.
