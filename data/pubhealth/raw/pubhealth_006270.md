# PubHealth Item 6270

Dataset source id: 6292
Document id: 26379

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
WhiteHouseGiftShop.com is selling COVID-19-themed commemorative coins for around $100. The gift shop has a historical connection with the White House that dates back to the Truman administration, but it was taken over by a private company. The federal government is not affiliated with the shop and isn’t involved in any of its products or operations, its owner says.
