# PubHealth Item 7087

Dataset source id: 7111
Document id: 26758

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
The Obama administration declared swine flu, or H1N1, a public health emergency six weeks before H1N1 was declared a pandemic. No H1N1 deaths had yet been recorded in the United States. Six months after that initial declaration, when more than 1,000 deaths had occurred, Obama himself declared H1N1 a national emergency.
