# PubHealth Item 8791

Dataset source id: 8818
Document id: 29243

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
The bottom line is that none of the supposed justifications for avoiding tilapia or claiming that “it’s killing our families” hold water. To the contrary, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) included tilapia in its 2017 list of “best choices” for seafood consumption by pregnant women and children, recommending two to three servings per week. The FDA also recommends eating a variety of fish, obviating the need to worry about balancing one’s intake of fatty acids.
