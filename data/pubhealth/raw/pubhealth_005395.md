# PubHealth Item 5395

Dataset source id: 5408
Document id: 25790

Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
Seneca Village was located in what later became Central Park, and many of the earliest residents were African Americans. The village was evicted through eminent domain before the park was built. It’s worth noting that Irish and German immigrants were also residents of Seneca Village and that Seneca Village only accounted for a fraction of what became Central Park. However, the gist of the story is accurate
