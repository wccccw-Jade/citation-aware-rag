# PubHealth Item 3756

Dataset source id: 3766
Document id: 30523

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: Students at the Mason Classical Academy in Naples, Florida, did rank highly among schools in the local district for English Language Arts and Mathematics in 2017, and their scores improved from the year before. What's false: The school never adopted Common Core in the first place, so the improvement in results was not linked to Common Core. In Mathematics, four public schools that participate in Common Core ranked above the Mason Classical Academy.
