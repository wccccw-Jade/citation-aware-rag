# PubHealth Item 9395

Dataset source id: 9422
Document id: 35322

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: The Woodstock music festival took place in August 1969. The H3N2 influenza pandemic lasted between the winter of 1968 and the early months of 1970. What's false: The 1968 pandemic does not compare to the COVID-19 pandemic in terms of lethality. Also, Woodstock did not take place during one of the Pandemic's seasonal peaks, but in the summer of 1969 when there was practically no reported H3N2 flu activity in the United States.
