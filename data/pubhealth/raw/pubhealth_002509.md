Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
"""Lopez said, """"As president, (Reagan) banned ownership of fully automatic rifles. He supported the assault weapons ban."""" Lopez errs in saying Reagan banned ownership of machine guns. Reagan, in 1986, signed a law that barred the private sale and ownership of machine guns that were not already registered on the day he signed the bill. The measure stopped new machine guns from entering the private marketplace, it did not ban the old ones. Lopez is correct on the second part of his claim. In 1994, after his presidency, Reagan signed a letter urging the House to pass an assault weapons ban."""
