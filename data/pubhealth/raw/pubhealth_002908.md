# PubHealth Item 2908

Dataset source id: 2918
Document id: 28514

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: The DEA recently approved a new oral liquid formulation of dronabinol — a synthetic version of the active ingredient in marijuana — as a schedule II drug. What's false: This is not the first time the DEA has approved dronabinol, which is wholly different than K2, Spice, and other recreational “synthetic cannabis” products.
