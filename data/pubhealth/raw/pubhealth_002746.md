But this all-but-two assessment also rests on how you define a mass shooting or gun-free zone. The statement is partially accurate but leaves out important details or takes things out of context. CLARIFICATION, 3:35 p.m., Jan. 13, 2016: We clarified Lott's reference to places that rarely grant civilian gun permits to indicate some jurisdictions don't grant them at all.
