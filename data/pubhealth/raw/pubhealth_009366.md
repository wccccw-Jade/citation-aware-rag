# PubHealth Item 9366

Dataset source id: 9393

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
Schiff, who now controls one of the Congressional committees overseeing an investigation into Russian interference in the 2016 U.S. presidential election, is the frequent target of conspiracy theorists. In February 2018, a false rumor claimed that Schiff’s sister was married to billionaire philanthropist George Soros’s son, but Schiff has no sister. Soros, who is Jewish, is also the frequent target of anti-Semitic conspiracy theorists.
