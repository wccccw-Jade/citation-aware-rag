# PubHealth Item 4568

Dataset source id: 4578
Document id: 29341

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: A 2014 study conducted in Japan examined formaldehyde levels in electronic cigarette vapor; early and inaccurate summaries of that research led to rumors that e-cigarettes contain ten times more carcinogens than tobacco cigarettes. What's false: A Japanese study did not conclude all, most, or necessarily any e-cigarettes contain ten times more carcinogens than regular cigarettes.
