Evidence and explanation:
N.C. Gov. Roy Cooper faced pushback for loosening restrictions on retail stores but not worship services. Cooper's reasoning: sitting around others for long periods of time is more dangerous than walking by someone in a store. While the world's top health organizations haven't compared those exact scenarios, experts generally agree with him.
