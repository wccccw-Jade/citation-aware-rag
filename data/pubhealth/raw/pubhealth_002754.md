# PubHealth Item 2754

Dataset source id: 2764
Document id: 26071

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
Gov. Newsom has broad authority under the California Emergency Services Act to issue health mandates during a pandemic. Two constitutional law experts said Newsom does, indeed, have the power to make face coverings mandatory under that act. They said unless face coverings are shown to be ineffective, Newsom’s requirement should hold up against legal challenges. Top public health officials and recent studies say wearing a face covering is an effective measure for reducing the spread of COVID-19 when people gather.
