# PubHealth Item 9217

Dataset source id: 9244
Document id: 26291

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
Amounts paid to hospitals can vary based on the payer; private insurers typically pay much more than Medicare. Medicare pays hospitals based primarily on the diagnosis, and the amount is not affected by whether a patient dies. The $17,500 corresponds to an average of what Medicare might pay for one type of COVID-19 patient. Experts said no hospitalization would generate a payment as low as $750.
