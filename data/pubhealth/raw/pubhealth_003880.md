# PubHealth Item 3880

Dataset source id: 3890
Document id: 29055

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: CFLs contain mercury, a potentially dangerous substance that escapes from broken CFLs into the immediate surroundings, and therefore the breakage of a CFL bulb should be handled by carefully removing the broken bulb and its contents from a home. What's false: The amount of mercury contained in one CFL bulb poses a grave danger to a home’s inhabitants, and the mercury dispersed by one broken CFL bulb needs to be dealt with only by an environmental clean-up crew.
