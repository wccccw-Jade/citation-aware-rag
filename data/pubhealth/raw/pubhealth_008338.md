# PubHealth Item 8338

Dataset source id: 8362
Document id: 16583

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
Medved said that police kill more whites than blacks. In absolute terms, that is accurate. However, the statement ignores that there are more than five times more whites than blacks in America. When comparing death rates, blacks are about three times more likely than whites to die in a confrontation with police. Medved’s statement leaves out significant information that would change someone’s understanding of the topic.
