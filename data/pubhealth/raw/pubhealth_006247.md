# PubHealth Item 6247

Dataset source id: 6269
Document id: 29180

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: The Trump administration FDA pushed back required start dates for agricultural water testing. What's false: Agricultural water testing regulations were delayed and not overturned, President Trump himself did not order the rollback of testing dates, the cause of the autumn 2018 E. coli outbreak has not been determined, and agricultural water testing would not have begun everywhere even if the original start dates had not been pushed back.
