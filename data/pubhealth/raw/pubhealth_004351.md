"""Citizen Action says """"people who come to"""" their dissent actions """"get a ‘cease and desist’ letter"""" from Johnson's office. And Johnson’s staff says it issued the letter, on the advice of the U.S. Capitol Police, because the man had been harassing and threatening to staff, not because he protested any of Johnson’s actions."""
