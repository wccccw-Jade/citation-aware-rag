# PubHealth Item 7574

Dataset source id: 7598
Document id: 25726

Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
COVID-related rates of hospitalization and death for children are lower than those of seasonal influenza. The risk of complications for healthy children is also higher for flu than for COVID-19, but infants and children with underlying medical conditions are at increased risk for both flu and COVID-19. Though the number of coronavirus cases among children has been relatively low, experts caution this could be because they have been at home and not in school. Experts also caution that children could be spreaders of the virus, and many unknowns remain.
