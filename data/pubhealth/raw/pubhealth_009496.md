Evidence and explanation:
Both stories had overall strengths, but both missed the cost question and could have done more to explain both harms and benefits. In all, though, they took a dispassionate look at studies that easily could have been hyped. Whenever you have a drugmaker moving into the naming phase on a product line, you know the marketing machine is getting revved up. That’s what is happening with Esmya. This story attempts to explain both the science behind the drug and the marketing push. With a little harder look at the science and a little better explanation of the studies’ ties to the drugmakers, the story could have been stronger.
