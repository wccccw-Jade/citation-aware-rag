# PubHealth Item 2784

Dataset source id: 2794
Document id: 26317

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
“Deaths per 100,000 people” is a public health metric used to quantify the intensity of an epidemic. In this case, it shows how many people have died from COVID-19 while controlling for countries’ populations. Although the U.S. is showing a lower number than countries such as Spain and Italy, various datasets indicate that, per capita, the U.S. has far more COVID-related deaths than does Germany and many other countries, including Canada, South Korea, Iran, Russia, Poland and Switzerland.
