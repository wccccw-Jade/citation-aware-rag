# PubHealth Item 5320

Dataset source id: 5333
Document id: 10493

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
This story described the results of a recent study which found that more intense treatment of acute kidney injury resulted in neither improved nor worsened outcomes. The story provided factual information about the study. It also indicated that there could be cost savings to the health care system if additional treatment which failed to provide benefit was avoided. However, the story failed to tell readers: whether both intensities of treatment are in general use and if not, which represented usual care; costs of individual treatment; anything beyond what appeared in the New England Journal of Medicine. There was no evidence of any new interviews or any reporting. That’s not the best form of health care journalism.
