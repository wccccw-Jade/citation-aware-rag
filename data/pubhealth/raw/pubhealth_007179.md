# PubHealth Item 7179

Dataset source id: 7203
Document id: 29106

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
What's true: Several published cases have documented the development of acute renal failure in dogs who consumed large quantities of grapes or raisins. What's false: Many dogs ingest grapes or raisins with no harmful effects, and the scientific mechanism linking grapes and raisins to acute renal failure in canines is unknown.
