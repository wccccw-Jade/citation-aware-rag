# PubHealth Item 4346

Dataset source id: 4356
Document id: 26570

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
A Pennsylvania congressman said the spread of the coronavirus in the U.S. matches the spread of the virus in South Korea, not Italy. The U.S. and South Korea have similar case fatality rates – Italy’s is higher – but all three countries have age-specific mortality rates that are about the same. Public health experts say the fatality rate wouldn’t be the only or the most important metric used to determine when businesses may reopen.The growth in cases and hospitals’ capacity to treat new patients is the most important factor.
