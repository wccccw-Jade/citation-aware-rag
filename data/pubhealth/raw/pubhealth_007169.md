# PubHealth Item 7169

Dataset source id: 7193
Document id: 11453

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
Look at that headline – “Coffee buzz protects brain from Alzheimer’s.” Think of how many coffee stories you’ve seen. Think of how many Alzheimer’s stories you’ve seen. Then appreciate that this work was in mice – and that the story goes out of its way to not only pump up the mouse findings but to project about human data that no one has yet seen. And then you can understand why readers get turned off to preliminary research stories that hype extremely preliminary findings.
