# PubHealth Item 7933

Dataset source id: 7957
Document id: 30036

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
Despite its fictional narrative, the basic premise of the article may well be worth heeding. Medical experts confirm that E. coli bacteria (and other pathogens) can be transmitted via oral-to-anal contact. The Centers for Disease Control (CDC) recommend using a dental dam for oral sex that involves either vaginal or anal contact.
