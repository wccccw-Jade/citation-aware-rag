# PubHealth Item 3883

Dataset source id: 3893
Document id: 16574

Fact-check label: true

Evidence and explanation:
Williams said that the No. 1 cause of death for black men 15-34 years old is murder. CDC data supports the claim. Out of all causes of death, homicide claimed about 40 percent of black lives between 15 and 34 years. This was significantly higher than the national average for males of that age group, and all other racial groups.
