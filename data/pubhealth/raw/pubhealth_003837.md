# PubHealth Item 3837

Dataset source id: 3847
Document id: 11473

Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
This is a story reporting on a recently published study comparing the health of infants born to mothers who did or did not receive DHA supplements during a later portion (i.e. starting at 18 – 22 weeks) of their pregnancy. These results follow several other publications about these infants and how they compare, which is something that should have been included in the story to provide readers with a more complete picture about what is known. The health benefits were reported as percent improvement rather than absolute differences, which means that it is not possible for readers to know the magnitude of the differences in order to consider whether they are meaningful or not. While minimizing illness in one’s offspring is an issue of concern to all parents, helping readers to understand the impact of an internvention is critical to their being able to make an informed decision about whether something is worth doing. And full discussion of risk is especially important in considering any supplement during the prenatal period.
