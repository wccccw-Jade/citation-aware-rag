Evidence and explanation:
"""Rubio, through a spokeswoman, said, """"Patrick Murphy is the only candidate to have voted against every measure to fund Zika."""" Murphy, like most congressional Democrats, has opposed Republican-led efforts to conditionally fund anti-Zika efforts at less than what Obama has requested. But he has offered other solutions and voted against his party for two bills that would have allowed unspecified amounts of money to be repurposed for Zika programs. So the campaign was wrong to say he voted against """"every"""" measure."""
