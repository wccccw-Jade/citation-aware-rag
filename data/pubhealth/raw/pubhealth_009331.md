# PubHealth Item 9331

Dataset source id: 9358
Document id: 16749

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
"""Gingrey said that amid the """"crisis at our southern border,"""" there are """"reports of illegal migrants carrying deadly diseases such as … Ebola virus."""" We don’t dispute that the conditions for newly arrived migrant children pose public health challenges. But Gingrey went well beyond that when he invoked Ebola, a particularly scary and untreatable disease with high mortality rates. The reality is that Ebola has only been found in Africa -- and experts agree that, given how the disease develops, the likelihood of children from Central America bringing it to the U.S. border is almost nonexistent. But most importantly for our fact-check, Gingrey’s office was unable to point to solid evidence that that Ebola has arrived in Western Hemisphere, much less the U.S. border. To the contrary, the CDC and independent epidemiologists say there is zero evidence that these migrants are carrying the virus to the border."""
