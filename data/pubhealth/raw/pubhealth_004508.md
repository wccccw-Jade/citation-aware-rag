Fact-check label: mixture

Evidence and explanation:
"""Cruz said, Obama’s net neutrality proposal """"puts the government in charge of determining Internet pricing, terms of service and what types of products and services can be delivered."""" While theoretically possible, Obama said specifically that he does not want the FCC to regulate Internet prices, and the FCC typically does not regulate prices for similar things, such as telephone services. In terms of product offerings, the proposal would allow the FCC to prevent Internet service providers from giving certain websites priority over others. But Cruz’s statement conjures an image of FCC officials sitting around a table designing Internet service packages and their prices, and this is not what Obama is proposing."""
