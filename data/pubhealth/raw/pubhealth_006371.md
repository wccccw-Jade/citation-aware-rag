# PubHealth Item 6371

Dataset source id: 6393
Document id: 10530

Fact-check label: false

Evidence and explanation:
The story summarizes two articles published in a peer review journal. One of the articles present the results of a randomized clinical trial of the use of tereparatide in the treat of osteonecrosis of the jaw related to severe periodontal or gum disease. The other is a case report of the use of teriparatide in the treatment of osteonecrosis of the jaw in an elderly women treated with Fosamax. Osteonecrosis is thankfully relatively rare but it does occur with some frequency in cancer patients so a new treatment regimen is of interest.
